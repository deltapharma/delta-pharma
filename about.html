About Delta Pharma

A local manufacturer with a simple aim: dependable medicines, produced to documented standards.

Our story

Delta Pharma was established in Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, to supply affordable, consistent medicines to the region and beyond. We invest in people, equipment and procedures so that quality is built into manufacturing rather than checked in at the end.
