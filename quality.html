Manufacturing and quality

Our quality system is built on current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) principles.

From material to market

  1. Material approval

    Suppliers are qualified. Incoming materials are quarantined, sampled and tested before use.

  2. Controlled production

    Batches follow approved master formulas, with line clearance and in-process checks.

  3. Testing and release

    Finished products are tested against specifications. Only approved batches are released.

  4. Traceability

    Batch records and retained samples allow any product to be traced and, if needed, recalled.
